                     Outil 'Magie' de Tux Paint : Briques

                                Groupe: Peindre

                 Auteur : Albert Cahalan <albert@users.sf.net>

These two tools intelligently paint large and small brick patterns on the
canvas. The bricks can be tinted various redish hues by selecting different
colors in the color palette.

This tool is operated in a freehand fashion.

This tool offers multiple size settings.
