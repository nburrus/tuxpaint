Tux Paint "Magic" Tool: Color Sep.

Tux Paint "Magic" Tool: Color Sep.

Group: Color Filters

Author: Bill Kendrick <bill@newbreedsoftware.com>

This separates your picture's colors. Selecting different colors can alter how they are separated: red from cyan (which can be used to create anaglyphic pictures you can view with 3D glasses), green from purple, or blue from yellow. Click and drag to adjust how far apart, and in what direction, separation occurs.
